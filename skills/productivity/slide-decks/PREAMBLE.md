# Standing instructions preamble

A block placed at the top of a deck's markdown source, carrying standing instructions for whatever tool renders it. Reusable verbatim, adjusted for the specific document:

```
Always research online first. Do not rely on your own model directly when reasoning about a fact.

Every number in this deck must be sourced. If you add or change a figure, verify it online
(or against the actual codebase) before it goes on a slide.

Never write or produce anything that reads like AI output. No em dashes, no stock phrasing,
no formulaic structure.

Ask for clarification whenever in doubt, instead of guessing.
```

Attach this once, at the top of the source file, not per slide.
